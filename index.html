Statistics

  • Mean: \(\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x}{n}\)
  • Median: middle value(s) of the ordered data
  • Mode: most frequent value(s)
  • Range: maximum − minimum
  • IQR: \(Q_3 - Q_1\)
  • Variance: \(\frac{\sum (x - \bar{x})^2}{n}\)
  • Std. deviation: \(\sigma = \sqrt{\text{variance}}\)
  • Standard score: \(z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}\)
  • Change of data: ×k then +c: mean ×k +c · range/IQR ×k · SD ×k · variance ×k² · z unchanged
